# Live optical cube recognition on the simulated shelf

Three bounded WireOS startup runs used the browser shelf scene, a docked start and the shapes renderer. The ordinary camera relay supplied the moving head image. The diagnostic ARM engine logged its decisions and saved grayscale frames at the actual marker-detector call. Capturing PNGs under QEMU costs wall time. These counts describe the instrumented runs only.

- Run 1 saved a 640 × 360 image and its 416 × 360 crop starting at x = 112, y = 0. The live detector returned one marker. The crop equals the corresponding rectangle in the full image pixel for pixel. Offline replay of both images using the upstream ARM checker returned MARKER_LIGHTCUBE_CIRCLE_BACK. The WebViz observedobjects subscription recorded zero cube observations. [Full image](full-000.png) · [Detector input](crop-000.png) · [Trace](run1.txt)
- Run 2 requested markers on ten frames. The rotation check excluded eight. Of the two remaining frames one crop was out of bounds and one reached the detector without a decode. [Trace](run2.txt)
- Run 3 requested markers on nine frames. All nine had bracketing IMU samples. Three exceeded the head threshold of 10°/s, one crop was out of bounds, and five reached the detector. The first three detector inputs produced no markers. The first saved frame was uniformly zero. Both later detections passed historical pose lookup. For timestamp 2864274778 the engine was on treads, had one raw object and retained it through filtering. It then skipped the unselected cube because its movement component classified the camera as moving at the time of processing or exposure. The trace does not distinguish which of those two checks fired. Offline decoding of the corresponding crop confirmed the back-face code. [First frame](run3-first-frame.png) · [First positive full frame](run3-detected-frame.png) · [Rejected cube crop](run3-last.png) · [Trace](run3.txt)

Trace fields follow the diagnostic calls in the patch. ROTTRACE records the firmware's rejection flag. IMUTRACE records the image timestamp, first and last history timestamps, availability of a bracketing pair and its two head and two yaw rates in rad/s. CROPTRACE records the index, image timestamp, crop x/y/width/height, charger-only flag and marker count. HISTTRACE records the pose lookup result (zero is success). MOVETRACE records the camera-moving and selected-object flags inside the branch that drops the observation. Run 1 recorded only vision scheduling and crops. Run 2 added the downstream decisions. Run 3 also recorded IMU history. [Diagnostic changes against 3cec4844](instrumentation.patch)
